About Us

We Love Dogs (And Clean Yards!)

Poopy Scoopy was founded right here in Milwaukee by dog lovers who understood a simple truth: everyone loves their pets, but nobody loves cleaning up after them.

What started as a simple idea has grown into Milwaukee's most trusted pet waste removal service. We treat every yard like it's our own, using a thorough grid-pattern cleaning method to ensure we never miss a spot.

Our team is made up of local pet enthusiasts who take pride in helping families reclaim their outdoor spaces. We're fully insured, professionally trained, and genuinely passionate about what we do.
